Our History

Over 13 Years of Coaching Excellence

Founded in 2013 in Pune, Maharashtra, Bharat Motor Driving School has grown from a single vehicle operation into a premier multi-branch driving academy. Our training methodology is centered around safety, patience, and complete compliance with motor vehicle regulations.

We believe that driving is a life skill that requires high-quality, professional coaching. Our custom dual-control vehicles and patient instructors guarantee a stress-free environment for beginners.
